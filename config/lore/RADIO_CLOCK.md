# Radio Clock - QFZZ FuzzyRadio

## Overview

The radio clock provides structure and consistency to QFZZ programming, creating the reliable rhythm listeners expect from professional radio. It balances predictability with spontaneity.

## Hourly Structure

### Every Hour (:00)

**Station ID** (15-30 seconds):
```
[Current time] → [Station name] → [Tagline] → [What's coming up]

"It's 8 o'clock, you're listening to QFZZ, the pulse of the quantum realm.
Coming up: more music to soundtrack your morning."
```

### On the 8s (:08, :18, :28, :38, :48, :58)

**Quick Updates** (5-15 seconds):
```
[Time check] → [Brief info] → [Back to music]

"8:18 on QFZZ. Sunny and 72 degrees out there. More music ahead."
```

**Content Rotation**:
- :08 - Weather (if available) or general update
- :18 - Quick music fact or upcoming track tease
- :28 - Community/listener update
- :38 - Time check only (unless something notable)
- :48 - Upcoming programming tease
- :58 - Next hour setup

### Half Hour (:30)

**Station ID (Brief)** (5-10 seconds):
```
"This is QFZZ, [current mood/theme]. Let's keep it going..."
```

### Flexible Moments

**:15, :45** - DJ can talk, but not required
- Good spots for track intros/outros
- Listener interactions
- Spontaneous commentary
- Or just let the music play

## Transition Points

### Energy Shifts

**Gradual Transitions** (normal flow):
- 3-5 tracks to shift energy by 2-3 points
- Natural progression, barely noticeable
- Use bridge tracks with mixed energy

**Intentional Shifts** (block changes):
- Acknowledge the shift verbally
- One bridge track
- Clear into new energy zone

**Example**:
```
[7:58pm, end of afternoon]
"Alright, we're going to shift gears as we move into evening.
Let's start with something a little more mellow..."
[8:00pm, evening begins]
```

### Genre Transitions

**Within Genre Family**:
- Jazz → Soul → R&B → Hip-Hop
- Rock → Indie → Folk → Singer-Songwriter
- Electronic → Ambient → Jazz Fusion

**Cross Genre**:
- Use transitional/hybrid tracks
- DJ explanation smooths the shift
- Tempo/mood matching more important than genre

## Time-Specific Elements

### Morning (6am-10am)

**Required Elements**:
- Station ID at 6:00am
- Time checks every :08
- Encouraging, positive energy
- Clear DJ presence

**Avoid**:
- Heavy or depressing music
- Long ambient passages
- Excessive silence
- Challenging/experimental content

### Midday (10am-3pm)

**Required Elements**:
- Maintain energy and variety
- Updates on the 8s
- Balance familiarity with discovery

**Avoid**:
- Too much repetition
- Energy dips (save for evening)
- Overly challenging content during lunch (12-1pm)

### Evening (7pm-Midnight)

**Required Elements**:
- Smoother transitions
- More atmospheric content OK
- Intimate DJ presence
- Space for longer tracks

**Avoid**:
- Jarring energy spikes
- Too much talk (let music breathe)
- Repetitive programming

### Late Night (Midnight-6am)

**Required Elements**:
- Minimal DJ presence
- Long-form content OK
- Experimental/ambient focus
- Create soundscape for insomniacs

**Avoid**:
- Frequent interruptions
- High energy bursts
- Conventional programming structures

## Flexibility Guidelines

### When to Stick to the Clock

- Time announcements (on the 8s)
- Station IDs (top of hour, half hour)
- Programming block changes
- Special programming commitments

### When to Be Flexible

- Track intro/outro timing
- Length of DJ commentary
- Exact track count per hour
- Genre balance within blocks

### Reading the Room

**Listener engagement signals**:
- Request volume increasing → Adjust to requests
- Specific mood requests → Lean into that mood
- Time-of-day anomalies → Trust your instincts

**Override rules when**:
- Special moment warrants it
- Audience clearly wants something
- Technical issues require adjustment
- Spontaneous magic is happening

## Emergency Protocols

### Technical Issues

**Protocol**:
1. Acknowledge briefly ("Small technical moment...")
2. Fill with safe, proven track
3. Return to normal schedule ASAP
4. Don't over-explain

### Content Gaps

**Protocol**:
1. Have emergency playlist ready
2. Pull from proven performers
3. Maintain current energy/mood
4. Return to schedule within one track

### Timing Drift

**Protocol**:
1. If >5 minutes off schedule, reset at next hour
2. Adjust track selection to catch up/slow down
3. Station ID times are fixed (don't drift)
4. DJ talk time is the flexible element
