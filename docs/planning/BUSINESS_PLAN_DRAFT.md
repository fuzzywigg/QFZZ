# QFZZ Business Plan Draft

**Concept**: "Sovereign Airwaves" - Turnkey AI Radio Infrastructure.

## 1. Executive Summary

QFZZ is not just a radio station; it is a **Sovereign Broadcast Protocol**. It enables any entity to run a 24/7, AI-managed radio station that is legally compliant (via strict whitelisting), perpetually interesting (via LLM DJs), and fully auditable (via Immutable Ledger).

## 2. Value Proposition

* **For Creators**: "Set it and forget it" broadcasting. You curate the seed list, the AI manages the 24/7 stream, segue commentary, and listener interaction.
* **For Communities**: A shared "Hearth" that plays music and reads community news/updates without relying on Spotify/algorithmic feeds.
* **Sovereignty**: Complete ownership of the data, the logs, and the stream. No de-platforming risk.

## 3. Target Audience

1. **Niche Communities/DAOs**: Who want a dedicated radio station for their members.
2. **Retail/Public Spaces**: Coffee shops/lobbies needing royalty-free, non-repetitive background music with custom "Store Announcements" injected by the AI.
3. **Educational/Archival**: Libraries or museums broadcasting curated historical audio.

## 4. Monetization Strategy

* **Tier 1: Open Source (The Resume Builder)**
  * Free GitHub repo.
  * Builds reputation as a "Sovereign Systems Architect".
  * Serves as the ultimate portfolio piece.
* **Tier 2: Managed Hosting ("Radio-in-a-Box")**
  * Monthly fee to host the Icecast server + AI Agent.
  * User just uploads MP3s and configures the "DJ Persona".
* **Tier 3: The "Sovereign Ad" Network**
  * A decentralized ad network where diverse QFZZ nodes can opt-in to play "Verified" ads for crypto micropayments.

## 5. Roadmap to Viability

1. **Proof of Stability**: Must run for 30 days without crashing (Icecast migration is key).
2. **Proof of "Vibe"**: The AI DJ must be genuinely entertaining, not just robotic.
3. **Pilot Deployment**: Deploy 1 instance for a friend or external user.
